How do these fit? +

True to size, cut a touch boxy through the chest. Built for a 30-something with shoulders, not a teenager with a skateboard. If you're between sizes, size down — the cotton relaxes about half a size after the first wash.

Where's it made? +

Cut, sewn, dyed, and embroidered in Brooklyn, New York. Cotton sourced from a single mill in North Carolina. We visit both, regularly. We can name the people who made your hat.

Will it shrink in the dryer? +

Hardly. Everything is garment-dyed and pre-washed before it ships, so what you see on the hanger is what you'll have after fifty washes. That said: cold wash, hang dry, and it'll outlive you.

What's your return policy? +

30 days. No grudge held. Send it back unworn (with the hangtag) for a full refund, or worn for a store credit. We've never argued about a return and we're not about to start.

Do you ship internationally? +

Yes — to the UK, EU, Canada, Australia, and Japan. Duties calculated at checkout. Anywhere else, drop us a line and we'll figure it out.
